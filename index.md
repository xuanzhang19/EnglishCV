### ***Biography***  
&emsp;Dr. **Xuan ZHANG** is an Associate Professor in the Department of Energy and Power Engineering, School of Mechanical Engineering, Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT). He received his B.S. and Ph.D. degrees from Beijing Institute of Technology and Tsinghua University (THU) in 2014 and 2019, and worked at Tsinghua University and Nanyang Technological University (NTU) as a Postdoctoral Researcher before joining Beijing Institute of Technology in 2022 as a member of **[<u>Frost Lab</u>](https://www.x-mol.com/groups/FrostLab)**.  

&emsp;Dr. ZHANG focuses on the mechanisms and applications of heat/mass transfer and fluid flow coupling processes, especially phase change problems (e.g., icing/frosting/freezing, condensation/evaporation/boiling, solidification/melting). He has published/co-authored 120+ SCI journal papers on Science, Nature Communications, Cell Reports Physical Science (x2), Advanced Science, Advanced Functional Materials, Nano Letters, Nano Energy, Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews (x2), Applied Physics Reviews, Fundamental Research, ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces, Chemical Engineering Journal, International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, Applied Thermal Engineering, Journal of Colloid and Interface Science, Physics of Fluids, Applied Physics Letters (with 60+ papers being first/corresponding authors and 12 selected as ESI Highly Cited/Hot, Featured, Editor’s Pick, Front Cover, and CNKI Highly Cited papers) and 7 books/chapters, given 10 invited reports in conferences, and applied for/authorized 20+ patents and 10+ software copyrights. His works have been cited by 4000+ times in Google Scholar with a h-index of 35 and have been reported 50+ times by famous science and technology media, such as 人民日报, 新华网, 中国科学报, The New York Times, EurekAlert! (a news website operated by the American Association for the Advancement of Science), Phys.org and Tech Xplore (two branches of Science X Network focusing on science and technology), Scienmag, and Bioengineer.  

&emsp;Dr. ZHANG has received 10+ research fundings from National Natural Science Foundation of China, Ministry of Science and Technology of China, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology of China, Beijing Municipal Natural Science Foundation, China Postdoctoral Science Foundation, Aeronautical Science Foundation of China, Open Fund of National Key Laboratory, etc. He has also been granted several talent programs, such as Young Elite Scientist Sponsorship Program by Beijing Municipal Association for Science and Technology, International Postdoctoral Exchange Fellowship Program, Postdoctoral Supporting Program by Tsinghua University, and listed among the **World’s Top 2% Scientists** by Stanford University/Elsevier (since 2023) and the **0.5% Top Scholars by ScholarGPS** Rankings (since 2025).  

&emsp;Dr. ZHANG has also received several scientific research awards, including the First Prize of Science and Technology Progress Award of Shandong Province, the Second Prize of the Science and Technology Progress Award of Chinese Society of Engineering Thermophysics, the Second Prize of the Science and Technology Progress Award of Chinese Society of Aeronautics And Astronautics, the Second Prize of the Technological Innovation Award of China Industry-University-Research Institute Collaboration Association, Wu Zhonghua Outstanding Graduate Student (Chinese Society of Engineering Thermophysics), Outstanding Doctoral Thesis (Tsinghua University), Highly Cited Doctoral Thesis (CNKI), and Best Presentation Awards (Chinese Association of Refrigeration, Chinese Society of Power Engineering, JSPS, Beijing Association of Refrigeration).  

&emsp;Dr. ZHANG has also served as the member of the Ice and Snow Technology Working Group of Chinese Association of Refrigeration, Associate Editor for Particulate Science and Technology (SCI, JCR Q3, Taylor & Francis), Editor Board Member for Scientific Reports (SCI, JCR Q1, Springer Nature), and Section Editor for Recent Patents on Mechanical Engineering (EI), as Early-Career/Youth Editor Members for Coatings (SCI, JCR Q2), Fluid Dynamics & Materials Processing (ESCI/EI/Scopus), Exploration (IF2024=22.5, ESCI/Scopus), Thermo (ESCI/Scopus), Clean Energy Science and Technology (Scopus), 工程科学学报(EI), 家电科技, etc., and as Guest Editors for Applied Thermal Engineering (SCI, JCR Q1, Elsevier), Micromachines (SCI, JCR Q2), Sensors (SCI, JCR Q2), Measurement & Control (SCI, JCR Q3), Energies (SCI, JCR Q3), Frontiers in Energy Research (SCI, JCR Q3), Thermo (ESCI/Scopus), AppliedMath (ESCI), etc., as Conference Committee Members for Frosting-Icing 2025, MSTP-2024、Frosting-icing 2024、CMAAE 2024、MEACM 2024、ICMEM 2023, and as Reviewers for 70+ academic journals.  

&emsp;Dr. ZHANG has taught various undergraduate and postgraduate courses, such as Information Retrieval and Utilization, Thermodynamics and Fluid Mechanics for Energy Storage, Information Retrieval and Scientific Writing, and Enhanced Heat Transfer in Intelligent Manufacturing. He has also led or participated in five teaching projects from the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology of China, Beijing Association for Science and Technology, and Beijing Institute of Technology. He has received several teaching awards, including the National First-Class Undergraduate Course (Ministry of Education of China), National Outstanding Online Open Course (Ministry of Education of China), Featured High-Quality Course for Engineering Masters and Doctors (Ministry of Industry and Information Technology of China), Outstanding Class Teacher (Beijing Institute of Technology), and Outstanding Teaching Assistant (Tsinghua University XuetangX).
